About me

Hello! I am Rivaldi Syaputra, an AI Prompt Engineer and IT Support Specialist based in Bandung, Indonesia. I design and refine prompts and system instructions so that AI models give accurate, consistent, and useful results.

My background is in computer systems and networking: Linux administration, Docker, CI/CD with GitHub Actions, Nginx and Apache, virtualization, and monitoring with Prometheus & Grafana. That foundation helps me build AI-assisted workflows that fit how real systems are deployed and operated.

In IT support, I have hands-on experience with hardware and software troubleshooting, user support, endpoint security, and network maintenance, keeping day-to-day operations smooth for teams and end-users.
